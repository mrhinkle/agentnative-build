## Strategy

1. **Own the category term.** "agent native" is essentially unclaimed (40/mo, KD 20). The home page is the definition: H1, title, first-sentence answer, FAQ schema. The goal is to be the cited source for "what does agent-native mean" in AI answer engines, not to chase volume.
2. **Ride the adjacent demand with winnable pages.** "mcp apps" (KD 38) and "conversational ui" (KD 34) are the realistic ranking targets. WebMCP (3.6K, KD 63) is a harder fight but is new, so freshness and primary-source citations matter.
3. **Question H2s with answer-first paragraphs** (40-60 words directly under the heading). This is the format answer engines lift.
4. **All content is static HTML.** JS only toggles; it never creates content.
5. **Machine surfaces:** /llms.txt, per-page .md twins, sitemap with lastmod, JSON-LD (WebSite, Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList).
6. **Cite primary sources** and mark uncertain items (WebMCP is a draft, not a standard).
